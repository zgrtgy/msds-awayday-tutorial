## Exercise 4: Process meeting notes {#exercise-4-process-meeting-notes}

::: {.callout-note collapse="true" appearance="minimal" icon=false title="Open exercise"}

**Scenario:** You have rough notes from a planning meeting and need to identify decisions and next steps.

**Use:** [03_simulated_meeting_notes.md](materials/03_simulated_meeting_notes.md).

**Try this prompt:**

```text
Turn these meeting notes into a short follow-up summary.

Separate confirmed decisions, action items (with owners and deadlines where stated), proposals that are not yet agreed, and unresolved questions. Do not invent responsibilities or turn suggestions into decisions.
```

**Then try:** Ask for a short follow-up email to the attendees based on the same notes.

**Check:** Did it separate confirmed decisions from suggestions? Are the people responsible and the deadlines supported by the notes?

:::
