## Exercise 7: Translate and simplify a message {#exercise-7-translate-and-simplify-a-message}

::: {.callout-note collapse="true" appearance="minimal" icon=false title="Open exercise"}

**Scenario.** You are preparing an English version of a Dutch announcement for international colleagues.

**Attach:** [07_simulated_dutch_announcement.md](materials/07_simulated_dutch_announcement.md).

**Try this prompt:**

```text
Translate the attached Dutch announcement into clear, natural English for international university staff.

Keep the dates, registration deadline, and distinction between confirmed and unconfirmed details exactly as in the original. Use friendly, straightforward language without adding new information.
```

**Then try:** Ask for a shorter version suitable for a newsletter or a plain-Dutch rewrite of the original.

**Check:** Did it preserve what is still uncertain? Would a colleague be able to act on the translated message?

:::
