## Exercise 10: Bring your own task {#exercise-10-bring-your-own-task}

::: {.callout-note collapse="true" appearance="minimal" icon=false title="Open exercise"}

**Scenario.** None of the exercises quite matches the task you would like to make easier.

**Choose a real *type of task*, but use fictional or approved non-sensitive example content.** For example: rewriting reminders, summarising guidance, making a checklist or checking the structure of a form.

**Prompt starter:**

```text
I often need to [DESCRIBE A ROUTINE TASK]. Here is a fictional example of the information I work with: [EXAMPLE].

Help me produce [DESIRED OUTPUT]. Keep these constraints in mind: [IMPORTANT RULES OR STYLE]. If essential information is missing, ask me rather than guessing.
```

**Check:** Would the output actually save you work after you review it? Which parts would still need a person to decide or verify?
