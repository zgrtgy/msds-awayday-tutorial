SIMULATED ROOM-BOOKING PROCEDURE — VERSION 1
TRAINING ONLY — NOT UU POLICY
Version 1.0 | Effective 1 July–31 October 2026

1. Standard room requests
Submit a room request at least 5 working days before an event. Include preferred date/time, expected attendance and room layout.

2. Hybrid/AV support
Request a technician at least 7 working days before the event if hybrid participation is needed. A standard projector and screen are included with a normal room booking; confirm availability when booking.

3. Catering
Send catering requests at least 10 working days before the event. Include the expected attendee count.

4. Changes and cancellations
Cancel a confirmed booking at least 2 working days before the event to avoid a possible cancellation charge.

5. Accessibility
Ask the facilities team separately about step-free access or other accessibility arrangements.
