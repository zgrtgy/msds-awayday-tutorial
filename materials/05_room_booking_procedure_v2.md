SIMULATED ROOM-BOOKING PROCEDURE — VERSION 2
TRAINING ONLY — NOT UU POLICY
Version 2.0 | Effective from 1 November 2026

1. Standard room requests
Submit a room request at least 7 working days before an event. Include preferred date/time, expected attendance and room layout.

2. Hybrid/AV support
Request a technician at least 10 working days before the event if hybrid participation is needed. A standard projector and screen are included with a normal room booking; confirm availability when booking.

3. Catering
Send catering requests at least 12 working days before the event. Include the expected attendee count and any relevant service requirements.

4. Changes and cancellations
Cancel a confirmed booking at least 3 working days before the event to avoid a possible cancellation charge.

5. Accessibility
Indicate accessibility requirements in the initial room request. Facilities will follow up about suitable arrangements.

6. Transition rule
Requests submitted before 1 November 2026 remain subject to version 1. Requests submitted on or after 1 November 2026 use version 2, even if the event takes place later.
