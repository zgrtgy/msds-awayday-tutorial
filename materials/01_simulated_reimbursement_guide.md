# Simulated staff expense reimbursement guide

**TRAINING MATERIAL — NOT UTRECHT UNIVERSITY POLICY**

Version 1.0 | Fictional Campus Operations Office | Effective 1 September 2026

## Purpose and scope

This simulated procedure applies to staff claiming expenses incurred while attending a one-day work-related event. It is intended for training only and is not Utrecht University policy. Expenses paid directly by the department must not also be claimed by an individual.

## 1. Eligible expenses

Second-class train and regular bus fares are eligible. A taxi is eligible only if written approval was obtained before the journey, for example because of an accessibility need or because public transport was unavailable. A single overnight hotel stay is eligible up to EUR 135 including tax; a higher amount requires written exception approval before booking. Lunch may be claimed up to EUR 20 and dinner up to EUR 35, but only if that meal was not already supplied by the event organiser. Alcohol is not eligible.

## 2. Documentation

An itemised receipt is required for every individual expense of EUR 10 or more. The claim must identify the claimant (staff reference, not a bank-account number), the event name and date, the cost centre, and the budget holder. The form should indicate whether a meal was supplied by the organiser. Do not put banking details or other sensitive personal data into the training materials.

## 3. Prior approvals

If the estimated total expense before the event exceeded EUR 250, the budget holder must approve the estimate before booking. This is separate from written approval required for taxi journeys and exceptions to the hotel cap. An approval should be recorded or attached to the claim.

## 4. Submission

Submit a complete claim within 30 calendar days after the last day of the event. Requests submitted later require an explanation and exceptional approval; they should not be treated as automatically accepted.

## 5. Unclear cases

Where a fact, exception or prior approval is not documented, ask the budget holder or the relevant administrative team for clarification. Do not treat missing information as evidence that approval exists.
